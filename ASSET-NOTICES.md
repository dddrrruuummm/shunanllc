## Generated visual assets

The client commissioned these service-presentation scenes using OpenAI ImageGen: fleet-hero-concept.jpg (black ceremonial formation), fleet-wedding-concept.jpg (white/silver wedding formation), fleet-memorial.jpg (dignified black limousine procession), fleet-chauffeurs.jpg (formal chauffeur presentation alongside a mixed luxury fleet). They are designed scenes rather than documentary company photographs. Public pages use the client's requested clean visual presentation without concept badges. The social preview is a copy of the commissioned ceremonial image.
